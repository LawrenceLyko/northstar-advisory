01 — Brand Introduction

Who We Are

Northstar Advisory is a strategic business consultancy that converts ambiguity into structured growth plans. We work with growth-stage ventures to build the frameworks, systems, and strategies that make scale possible.

08 — Brand Voice

Brand Voice & Tone

Calm, precise, never condescending. Full stops over exclamation marks. Sentences that land with weight, not volume.

01

Direct

Lead with the point. No meandering preamble. The answer, then the rationale.

❌ "In order to facilitate the process of strategic alignment across organizational stakeholders..."
✓ "Here is the plan. Here is why it works."
02

Precise

Use specific numbers, timeframes, and outcomes. Vague language signals uncertainty.

❌ "We'll grow your business significantly in a short period..."
✓ "Measurable results within 90 days. A working system that survives beyond the engagement."
03

Confident without arrogance

Quiet authority. We don't shout. We don't oversell. Certainty comes from evidence, not volume.

❌ "We are the BEST consultancy in the market!!"
✓ "We've seen this pattern before. Here's what works."
04

Human, not corporate

Speak to the person, not the organisation. Acknowledge complexity. Don't pretend it's easy.

❌ "Utilising synergistic methodologies to optimise holistic outcomes..."
✓ "Scaling is hard. We build the framework that makes it manageable."

09 — Brand Applications

Brand in Application

The Northstar Advisory identity across print, environmental, and physical touchpoints. Consistent, composed, unmistakably Northstar.

Print Collateral

Tube mailers, document rolls, and presentation packaging. Navy exterior with Electric Blue typographic labels.

Out-of-Home Advertising

Large-format applications use full-bleed Navy with Ivory headline typography. Mark scaled for distance legibility.

Brand Stickers & Labels

Adhesive labels and merchandise stickers. Clean die-cut on transparent or white grounds. Never on colour.

Icon System

1.5px line-weight icons in a consistent geometric style. Always monochrome — Navy on Ivory, Ivory on Navy. No fill icons.

11 — Motion Direction

Motion Direction

Purposeful, not decorative. Motion signals meaning — an entrance earns attention, a transition signals progress. No bounce. No elastic. Precision.

Timing

  • Logo draw-on: 250–400ms
  • UI transitions: 150–300ms
  • Page transitions: 400–500ms
  • Text stagger: 60–80ms per line

Easing

  • Primary: cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)
  • Entrances: EaseOut — decelerate in
  • Exits: EaseIn — accelerate out
  • ❌ Never bounce, elastic, or spring

Principles

  • Horizontal wipes for transitions
  • Directional slides for content
  • 8–12px upward slide for text
  • Sequential reveal, not simultaneous

Directional Star Draw-On Simulator

Watch the official star mark segments draw on sequentially — 250–400ms per stroke, EaseOut easing.

Line-by-Line Text Reveal

Text lines slide up 10px with 70ms stagger. EaseOut. Never all at once.
